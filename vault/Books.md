# Books

A book in jadeli is a manuscript made of ordinary notes, in order, with a word
target. Chapters stay normal markdown files — usually in `Books/<Book title>/`,
though a book can point at any folder in the vault. Nothing is hidden inside a
database.

## Working on a book

1. Open the **Books** tab in the left rail.
2. Pick a book to see the dashboard: chapters, total words, and progress to target.
3. Use **+ Chapter** to add one, or **Attach note** to pull an existing note into the manuscript.
4. Reorder with the arrows — the numeric filename prefixes are kept in sync (`01 -`, `02 -`).
5. **Export** combines the chapters into a single `.md` or print-ready `.html` in `Exports/`.

> [!tip] Two panes
> Press `Ctrl+Shift+\` to open a second pane. Put the chapter on one side and a
> character sheet or the outline on the other — they scroll and save independently.

## The demo manuscript

**[[The Lantern's Edge — Story Bible|The Lantern's Edge]]** lives in its own
project folder, `The Lantern's Edge/`: the chapters sit at the top level and the
world bible is in `The Lantern's Edge/Bible/`.

A keeper's granddaughter lights the last dark tower on the edge of a broken ring,
and every power on the coast comes to put it out.

The bible behind it:

- [[The Lantern's Edge — Story Bible]]

  in hours, and the caramel light in its case
- [[The Toll]] — the one Holding shaped as sound: its laws, its price, and its
  single use
- [[The Ladder]] — how power grows, rung by rung, and the price of each one
- [[The Coat and the Fire]] — Mira's rise, the litany, the mask, and the refusals
- [[Characters]] — wants, wounds, lies, arcs, romance beats
- [[Outline]] — five parts, thirty chapters, continuity ledger
- [[The Two of Them]] — the romance register, the counter-vow, and a dialogue bank
- [[Lines and Flair]] — the rogue, oath and elegiac registers, with sample lines
- [[Tone and Influences]] — how the requested flavours are used, and what to avoid

> [!note] Word targets
